Get up and running with Kore in a few simple steps.

First, add the Kore dependency to your project:

```kotlin
implementation("io.github.dev778g-me:kore:1.0.0-alpha03")
```

While Kore works perfectly out of the box using KoreDefaults, configuring your own design system is highly recommended.

1. Visit the [Kore Playground](/create/) to prototype your design system visually.

<Image src={homeDark} alt="Kore Playground Home" />

2. Customize your design system: tweak colors (neutrals, primary, and complementary accents), shapes, and spacing.

<Image src={createPage} alt="Customize Theme" />

3. Generate and export the Theme.kt file, then drop it into your project.

<Image src={exportDark} alt="Export Theme" />

4. Wrap your root composable with AppTheme (feel free to rename this inside the file).

That's it. All Kore components will now automatically adapt to your custom theme.

Happy building 😻
