<section class="kh-statement">
<h3>Material doesn’t fit every product</h3>
<div class="kh-copy">
<p>Not every app should look or feel like Material. Building your own design system from scratch takes time, design sense, and a lot of iteration.</p>
<p><strong>Kore gives you pre-styled Compose Multiplatform components out of the box.</strong> Customize as much as you want: colors (light and dark), shapes, typography, sizes, everything.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="kh-split">
<h3>From install to your own theme in 4 steps</h3>
<ol class="kh-steps">
<li><b>Install</b><span>One Maven Central dependency. Android, iOS, desktop, web.</span></li>
<li><b>Open <a href="/create/">Create</a></b><span>Prototype with live components, not mockups.</span></li>
<li><b>Export Theme.kt</b><span>Drop the generated file into your project.</span></li>
<li><b>Wrap your root</b><span>Every component adapts automatically.</span></li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="kh-split">
<h3>Why Kore</h3>
<dl class="kh-why">
<div><dt>One theme, fully yours</dt><dd>Colors for light and dark, shapes, type, and sizes live in tokens you own. Build them in Create and export <code>Theme.kt</code>, or write them by hand.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Styled from the first build</dt><dd>Components ship with finished defaults, so your first build already looks right. Restyle what you want later.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Parameters first, source second</dt><dd>Almost every visual detail is a parameter. When that isn’t enough, each component is a single file you can copy into your project and edit.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Accessible by default</dt><dd>Components are accessible out of the box, so restyling doesn’t mean redoing that part.</dd></div>
</dl>
</section>

<section class="kh-split">
<h3>Questions</h3>
<div class="kh-faq">
<details name="faq"><summary>Is Kore production-ready?</summary><p>No, it’s alpha. APIs may shift. Great for new projects and prototyping, not yet for frozen design systems.</p></details>
<details name="faq"><summary>Which platforms are supported?</summary><p>Android, iOS (arm64/x64), JVM desktop, Kotlin/JS and Kotlin/Wasm web targets.</p></details>
<details name="faq"><summary>How is this different from Material?</summary><p>Material is one opinionated system. Kore is a starting point you reshape. Every token and almost every parameter is yours to change.</p></details>
<details name="faq"><summary>Does it work with my existing theme?</summary><p>Yes. Wrap your root, export Theme.kt from Create, and components follow your colors, shapes, and type.</p></details>
<details name="faq"><summary>How much does it cost?</summary><p>Free and MIT licensed, forever.</p></details>
</div>
</section>
